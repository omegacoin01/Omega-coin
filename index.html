How Omega Coin works
Buying and withdrawing, step by step.
  1. Tap Buy coin and choose how many Omega you want. The price follows the current rate.
  2. Pay that amount into the bank account shown.
  3. Tap I have paid, then send a photo of your receipt with the camera button in the chat.
  4. The Account Manager confirms your payment and adds the coins to your balance.
  5. To withdraw, tap Withdraw and message the Account Manager.

The rate moves ₦100 for every 100 coins in circulation: up when coins are added, down when coins are removed. It is set by this rule, not by an open market.
